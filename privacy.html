2. What data we process and why

Booking the 60-minute DiagnosticName, email address, phone number, company and whatever you tell us when booking. We use them to organise the meeting and prepare your diagnostic.
Recording of the meetingWith your consent, we record the video call to prepare the written summary we give you. You can ask for it not to be recorded.
EmailsThe data you send us by email, in order to reply to you.
Lost Hours Calculator: answersAt calculadora.almudenagazquez.com we store the answers used for the calculation: company size, sector, how many people and how much time in each block, the result and the link you came from (for example, Instagram). We do not store a name or email address, so they do not identify anyone. We use them for statistics: to know which sectors use the calculator and where visitors come from.
Calculator: your details and the PDF reportTo see your result we ask for your name, your company and your email address and, if you wish, your phone number, together with the acceptance box (yes, date and the text you accepted). We use them to send you the PDF report by email and to contact you about your result, by email or, if you give it to us, by phone. Only if you tick the separate box "I want to receive ideas and resources about AI for companies by email", also to send you those emails; we store the date and the text you accepted. Without that box we do not send you advertising.
Instagram assistant: what it isOn Instagram (@almudena.gazquez.ia), private messages and some comments are answered by an automated AI assistant that introduces itself as "IA Almudena". When your query requires it, the assistant passes it to Almudena, who answers you in person.
Instagram assistant: data it processesYour Instagram username and identifier, the messages you send it and whether you follow the account (Instagram gives us these three items). If you ask for a free resource, your email address. If you agree to receive more resources, the date and the text you accepted.
Instagram assistant: what forFirst, to reply to your messages and email you the resource you ask for. Second, only if you accept with the "Sí, y más recursos" ("Yes, and more resources") button, to email you more resources from time to time. Receiving the resource does not depend on accepting more emails.
Ads on Facebook and Instagram: measurementOnly if you reach the calculator from one of our ads on Facebook or Instagram: when you request the report, we let Meta know you have requested it. We send it your email address, irreversibly encrypted ("hashed"), the identifier of the click on the ad, your IP address and your browser type. This is used to know which ads work and so that Meta can show them to similar people. We do not use the Meta pixel or its cookies. If you do not arrive from an ad, nothing is sent.

We do not use your data to send you advertising without your permission and we do not sell it to anyone.

The assistant does not make decisions with legal effects on you and does not create profiles. It only checks whether you follow the Instagram account in order to deliver the resource to you.

3. Legal basis

We process your data because you request it when booking or writing to us (steps taken before entering into a contract, art. 6.1.b GDPR) and, for the recording, because you give us your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time.

In the calculator, sending you the report and contacting you about your result is based on your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you give with the required box. Sending you ideas and resources by email is based on a separate consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR and art. 21 of the Spanish Information Society Services Act, LSSI), which you give only if you tick the optional box. You can withdraw either of them whenever you like with the unsubscribe link in each email or by writing to ag@almudenagazquez.com.

In the Instagram assistant, replying to your messages and sending you the resource you ask for is based on the fact that you request it (art. 6.1.b GDPR). Sending you more resources by email is based solely on your consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR), which you give with the "Sí, y más recursos" ("Yes, and more resources") button. You can withdraw it whenever you like with the unsubscribe link in each email or by writing to ag@almudenagazquez.com.

Notifying Meta when you arrive from an ad is based on our legitimate interest in measuring the effectiveness of our ads (art. 6.1.f GDPR). You can object whenever you like by writing to ag@almudenagazquez.com.

4. How long we keep it

Booking data and the summary, for as long as the business relationship lasts and, afterwards, for the legally required periods. Recordings are deleted within a maximum of 6 months if we do not end up working together.

Calculator data (name, company, email address, phone number if you give it and consents) is kept for 12 months from sending, unless you ask for it to be deleted sooner; if you agreed to receive ideas and resources, until you unsubscribe. The calculator's anonymous answers are kept with no time limit, because they are not personal data.

Instagram assistant data is kept for 12 months from your last message if you only asked for the resource. If you agreed to receive more resources, it is kept until you unsubscribe. After that, we keep only the proof of your consent and of your unsubscribing for as long as the law requires.
